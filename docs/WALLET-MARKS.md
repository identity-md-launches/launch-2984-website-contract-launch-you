# Wallet marks

The small MetaMask, Rabby and Phantom SVG marks in `public/wallets/` were copied from RainbowKit's wallet connector assets:

- https://github.com/rainbow-me/rainbowkit/tree/main/packages/rainbowkit/src/wallets/walletConnectors/metaMaskWallet
- https://github.com/rainbow-me/rainbowkit/tree/main/packages/rainbowkit/src/wallets/walletConnectors/rabbyWallet
- https://github.com/rainbow-me/rainbowkit/tree/main/packages/rainbowkit/src/wallets/walletConnectors/phantomWallet

The Coinbase mark was supplied by the task's pinned wallet connector reference. Marks retain their brand colors and identify their respective wallet providers; no endorsement is implied. RainbowKit is MIT licensed; the upstream license is retained in `docs/RAINBOWKIT-LICENSE.txt`.

The Rabby app link follows its public universal-link handler (`_cmd=open-dapp`, `dapp`): https://github.com/RabbyHub/rabby-mobile/blob/develop/apps/mobile/src/hooks/universalLink.ts.
